Install Formly (2 minutes)

Formly is in beta, so it installs a little differently from the Chrome Web Store. Just follow these 5 steps.

💻 Do this on your laptop in Google Chrome. Chrome on phones doesn't support extensions.
1

Download Formly

⬇️ Download Formly

Then open your Downloads folder and double-click formly-beta.zip to unzip it. You'll get a folder called Formly.

2

Open Chrome's extensions page

Copy this into Chrome's address bar and press Enter:

chrome://extensions

3

Turn on Developer mode

Flip the Developer mode switch in the top right corner.

4

Load Formly

Click Load unpacked (top left), choose the Formly folder you unzipped, and click Select.

5

Sign in and pin it

  • A welcome page opens. Sign in with the same email you signed up with. Your 3 free forms are waiting.
  • Click the 🧩 puzzle icon in Chrome, then the 📌 next to Formly.
  • Set up your profile: upload your resume and Formly fills it in for you.
🎉 Done! Open any application form (for example a Google Form) and press ✨ Fill with Formly.

Keep the Formly folder (don't delete it), or Chrome will remove the extension.
