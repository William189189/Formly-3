✨ Formly
How it works Safety Install GitHub

Fill in any form in seconds.

Formly is an AI Chrome extension for students. One click fills club sign-ups, event registrations and applications, including the "Why do you want to join?" answers. And it learns something new every time you submit.

⬇️ Download Formly View on GitHub

How it works

Three steps. You stay in control the whole time.

1

Tell Formly about you

Fill in your profile, or upload your resume or LinkedIn PDF and let the AI fill it for you.

2

Press ✨ on any form

Formly reads every question, finds the right facts and writes long answers from your real experience.

3

Check and submit

Blue boxes are filled, orange ones are for you. Formly never presses Submit for you.

🧠 Learns as you go

Type it once. Never again.

Form A asks for your Telegram username and Formly doesn't know it. You type it and press Submit. Formly saves it into the Contact section of your profile. On Form B, it's filled for you.

Learned boxes are tagged, show which website they came from, and can be edited or deleted any time.

Everything in one place

A full profile page that you control.

👤

Your profile, your way

Sections for personal, contact, education and more. Add your own boxes, delete any box.

📄

Upload PDF or Word

Resume, LinkedIn "Save to PDF", personal statements. Read on your computer, searched for the best parts.

🎯

Shows up only on forms

The button appears on real forms, not on Google Docs, Gmail or search pages. Switch it off any time.

🛡️ Responsible AI

Built to be careful

  • Never presses Submit. You always review first.
  • No made-up facts. The AI may only use your profile and documents. Guessed facts from resumes are thrown away by code.
  • Hands off secrets. Password boxes are never read. NRIC, passport, bank and card answers are never learned.
  • Consent is yours. "I agree" boxes are left for you to tick.
  • Never overwrites what you already saved, and learning can be switched off.
  • Stored on your computer. Data only goes to the AI provider you choose.

Install Formly

Free, open-source, takes about 3 minutes. You need Chrome and an OpenAI-compatible API key.

  1. Download Formly and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the client folder.
  4. Pin Formly from the 🧩 menu, click it → My Profile & Settings → Settings, paste your API key and press Test key.
  5. Fill in your profile (or upload your resume), open any form, and press ✨ Fill with Formly.
