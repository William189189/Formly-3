Formly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Formly is a Chrome extension that helps you fill in online forms. This page explains, in plain words, what information Formly collects, why, and what we do with it.

The short version

  • Your profile, documents and form answers stay on your own computer, in your browser.
  • Our server only keeps your email, Telegram username, and how many forms you fill.
  • When you press Fill, the form's questions and the relevant parts of your profile are sent through our server to an AI service to write the answers. We don't store them.
  • We never sell your information.

What we collect and why

Your profile, documents (resume etc.), and facts Formly learnsOnly on your computer (your browser's storage)So Formly can fill forms for you.
The form's questions, the page title and website name, and the parts of your profile and documents needed to answerNot stored. Passed through our server to the AI service, then discarded.To write the answers when you press Fill, or to tidy up new facts when you submit a form.

Things Formly never does

  • Never reads password boxes, and never learns NRIC, passport, password, bank or card details.
  • Never presses Submit on a form for you.
  • Never sells or rents your information, and never uses it for advertising.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase: hosts our server and database (email, Telegram username, usage counts).
  • Our AI provider (an OpenAI-compatible service): receives the questions and the profile parts needed to answer them, only when you use Formly.

Your choices

  • Delete your profile and documents: remove them in Formly → My Profile, or uninstall Formly.
  • Turn off learning: Formly → My Profile → Settings.
  • Delete your account (email, Telegram and usage counts) or ask what we hold about you: contact us below and we'll do it.

Keeping it safe

Our database can't be read from the internet. Only the Formly server and the Formly team can access it. Your sign-in token is stored scrambled, so even we can't see it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/William189189/Formly-3/issues.
